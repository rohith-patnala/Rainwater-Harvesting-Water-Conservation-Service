FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our water conservation services.

What is rainwater harvesting?

Rainwater harvesting is the process of collecting and storing rainwater from rooftops or other suitable surfaces for future use.

Do you provide site assessments?

Yes. Our site assessment service helps understand your property, water requirements, available space, and suitable solutions.

Is rainwater harvesting suitable for homes?

Rainwater harvesting can be planned for homes depending on rooftop area, rainfall, storage space, and water requirements.

What is a recharge pit?

A recharge pit is a planned structure that helps rainwater move into the ground and support groundwater recharge.

How does a water audit help?

A water audit helps identify water usage, possible wastage, and opportunities to improve water efficiency.

Can businesses use your services?

Yes. Our services can be planned for homes, offices, commercial buildings, and communities based on their requirements.
