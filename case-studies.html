Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Water Conservation

Find answers to common questions about rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, and water conservation solutions.

What is rainwater harvesting?

Rainwater harvesting is the process of collecting and storing rainwater from rooftops or other suitable surfaces for future use, recharge, or conservation.

Why is rainwater harvesting important?

It helps reduce water wastage, improve water availability, support groundwater recharge, and reduce dependency on external water sources.

Can rainwater harvesting be installed in existing buildings?

Yes. Rainwater harvesting systems can be planned for existing homes, apartments, offices, commercial buildings, and other suitable locations based on site conditions.

What is a groundwater recharge pit?

A groundwater recharge pit is a specially designed structure that allows collected rainwater to enter the ground and support groundwater replenishment.

Do you provide site assessment services?

Yes. We assess the location, rooftop area, water requirements, available space, and site conditions before suggesting a suitable water conservation solution.

How can I start my water conservation project?

You can contact our team through the Contact page or book a site assessment to discuss your requirements and project planning needs.
