Pricing Details

What Determines The Cost?

Several factors are considered before preparing the final project quotation.

Property Size

The size of the rooftop and collection area affects the overall system design and cost.

Storage Capacity

The required tank size and water storage capacity influence the final project price.

Filtration System

The type of filter and water purification requirements can change the total cost.

Installation Work

Installation complexity, labour, and site conditions are included in the quotation.
